About UF

Ideas Archive & Creative Sanctuary

UF is a curated archive of digital experiments, humane tools, and tactile concepts created over the years. I believe technology feels best when it is built like a warm, sunlit kitchen — forgiving, thoughtful, and scented with quiet joy. Here you will find concepts ranging from cozy journaling apps to sustainable marketplaces and atmospheric indie games.

Warmth First

Software that respects human attention, forgives broken streaks, and brings calm into daily rituals.

Story-Driven

Every prototype starts as a story, memory, or hand-bound sketch before ever becoming code.
